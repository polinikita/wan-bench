# paper-fixedbuild-20260822 — figure data for the signature-free paper

The complete measurement record behind `figures/{committee-scaling,throughput-sweep,
throughput-sweep-overload,leader-relay}.dat` as regenerated by
`vantage/gen_paper_dat.py` (the `.dat` files, their per-cell `provenance.json`,
and the derived `carrier-cadence.json` are under `figures/`; the contributing
`sweep.json`/`campaign.json`/`matrix.json` trees are under `measurements/`,
without full per-node Prometheus archives).

`carrier-cadence.json` was derived before the full Prometheus scrapes were
pruned from this compact record.  It preserves the median node counter delta,
actual window, and derived rate for each available n=100, 100 tx/s run.  Vantage
and the DAG baselines expose progress gauges directly.  Autobahn slot rates
divide received `ConsensusRequest` deltas by one request per optimistic slot or
two per seamless slot.  The units describe fresh-reference opportunities and
are not equivalent protocol work.
